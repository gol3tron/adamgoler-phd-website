The idea

Quantitative decisions compound.

The choice of model, the metric you optimize, the experiment you trust, the assumption you never tested — these decisions compound exactly like technical debt, except they're harder to see. A dashboard looks fine while the number underneath it quietly means nothing.

I'm a PhD physicist who has spent a decade turning messy, real-world data into systems people actually bet on — production ML across thousands of aircraft, LLM agents in daily use, and safety research where being wrong has consequences. A fractional CSO gives you that judgment embedded in your team, at the stage where it changes the trajectory — without a full-time hire.

Is this for you?

Who this is for

I'm deliberately selective — the engagement only works when the fit is real. Here's how to tell.

✅ Strong fit

  • Your product's value depends on getting the math, modeling, or ML right.
  • You have data but aren't sure you're drawing trustworthy conclusions from it.
  • You need senior quantitative judgment — but not yet a full-time science hire.
  • Your team can build; they need direction on methods, rigor, and evaluation.
  • You want to grow quantitative capability in-house, not just buy a deliverable.

🚫 Not a fit

  • You need a full-time engineering manager or pure delivery lead.
  • You want someone to rubber-stamp decisions that are already made.
  • The work has no real quantitative core — it's app plumbing and UI.
  • You're shopping for the cheapest possible contractor hours.
  • You need 40 hours a week of hands-on coding.

How it works

The arc of an engagement

Designed to add the most value early — and to work itself out of a job.

  • 1 · Diagnostic

    A short, paid engagement to map where you are, what you're building, and where the quantitative risk really sits. If it's not a fit, you'll know quickly.

  • 2 · Foundations

    Set direction and fix the highest-leverage gaps — the model, the metric, the evaluation, the data — so the things you build next stand on something solid.

  • 3 · Embedded

    Ongoing fractional involvement: a set rhythm each month, hands-on where it matters, a thought partner where it counts.

  • 4 · Enablement

    Build your team's capability — coach the people you have, and help you hire the ones you need.

  • 5 · Transition

    Hand off cleanly to a full-time science or technical leader when you're ready for one. A natural exit is the goal, not a surprise.

The model

Aligned on outcomes — not billable hours.

The goal is alignment, not maximizing short-term fees. Structure flexes to your stage:

Retainer

A set number of days each month at a predictable rate. Best for funded teams that need senior quantitative judgment on a recurring basis.

Cash + equity

For earlier-stage startups: a modest monthly cash component plus a meaningful equity stake that vests over time and milestones.

Fixed-fee

For a diagnostic, workshop, or scoped sprint: a flat price for a defined outcome, agreed up front. No open-ended meters.
