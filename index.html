About Me

I'm a physicist by training and a builder by practice. Over the past decade I've taken technical systems from idea to production — most recently as a software engineer and data scientist building applied-AI and machine-learning programs in aviation.

At Savvy Aviation I lead two production ML programs — FEVA and GADfly — that flag developing engine problems before failure, together producing 58,000+ analyses across 7,400+ aircraft, and I design LLM agents that let non-technical users get grounded, checkable answers from messy data. As Director of the Savvy Aviation Foundation, I lead research applying LLM classification and survival analysis to NTSB accident data.

I also serve as a Major in the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve's Marine Innovation Unit, evaluating emerging technology, and instruct pilots as an FAA Certified Flight Instructor. A PhD in physics, semiconductor R&D, and military leadership give me a multidisciplinary lens on every problem.
