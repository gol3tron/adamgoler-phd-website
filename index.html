About Me

Physicist by training, builder by practice. A decade taking technical systems from idea to production — most recently applied AI and machine learning in aviation.

At Savvy Aviation I build production-grade, customer-facing ML and LLM systems for applications critical to aviation safety. They flag developing engine problems before failure and give operators grounded, checkable answers from messy data. As Director of the Savvy Aviation Foundation, I lead research applying LLM classification and survival analysis to NTSB accident data.

I'm also a Major in the U.S. Marine Corps Reserve's Marine Innovation Unit, evaluating emerging technology, and an FAA Certified Flight Instructor. Physics PhD, semiconductor R&D, military leadership — a multidisciplinary lens on every problem.
