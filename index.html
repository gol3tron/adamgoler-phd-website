What I'm Working On

The day job, the side projects, and the stuff in between.

Day job · Savvy Aviation

Catching engine problems early

Production ML and LLM systems that spot developing engine trouble in piston aircraft before it becomes a failure. 58,000+ reports across 7,400+ aircraft so far.

Research · Savvy Aviation Foundation

What the accident record says

Using LLM classification and survival analysis on NTSB accident data. The goal: find the patterns that matter and turn them into safer flying.
